# Where these marks came from

- `airbnb.svg`, `hilton.svg` - Simple Icons (https://simpleicons.org), CC0. The files are
  public domain; the trademarks they depict remain Airbnb's and Hilton's. Recoloured white
  here because the story samples them as dot masks, not as brand artwork.
- `leland.png` - Leland's own mark, resolved from joinleland.com. Dark on a pale ground, so
  it is inverted before sampling.

think[box] and OpsiClear have no mark of their own to use: think[box] sets its name as type,
brackets and all, and OpsiClear appears to have none at all. Both are drawn as their names.

Used only to identify real employers in the experience chapter, never to imply endorsement.
